## The spec

Wheels **18 inch or larger** (19 or 20 preferred, but the Meridian's 18-inch
all-terrains are acceptable), exterior **Cypress green** or **Polymetal /
Machine Gray**, interior **terracotta** ideally, **black with brown**
acceptable, **any engine at the right price**.

The wheel rule is a trim filter, not a styling note. It still removes every
17-inch car, which includes the Hybrid Premium, a trim "Premium or better"
would otherwise admit:

| Trim | Wheels | In spec |
|---|---|---|
| 2.5 S Select, S Preferred | 17 inch | no |
| 2.5 S Meridian, Turbo Meridian | 18 inch, all-terrain tires | yes |
| Hybrid Preferred, **Hybrid Premium** | 17 inch | no |
| 2.5 S Premium | 20 inch | yes |
| 2.5 Turbo, Turbo Premium Plus | 20 inch | yes |
| Hybrid Premium Plus | 19 inch | yes |
| CX-70 (any 3.3) | 21 inch | yes |

Terracotta comes on the **Turbo Meridian** (18 inch), the **Turbo Premium
Plus** (20 inch) and, the useful surprise, the **base 2.5 Turbo** (20 inch),
which stickers about $5,000 under the Premium Plus. It never comes on any
hybrid or on any 2.5 S.

## Financing

Customer cash and Mazda's special APR do not combine on the Turbo and the
CX-70: it is $3,000 (or $3,500) **or** 0% for 36 months. On the 2.5 S
Premium and the Hybrid Premium Plus, September's program allows up to
$1,500 cash **and** 0% for 36; confirm it on the specific VIN, because
dealer summaries disagree. With roughly $20,000 down the two are close, so
take the cash and a credit union loan (4.9 to 5% new, about 5.75% used) on
the Turbo, and the combo on the S Premium or the hybrid. If 0% is real,
put down as little as they allow and leave the cash earning 4%.

## Certified: skip it on the CX-50

Thirteen certified cars within 300 miles have big wheels. The two that
match the colour and interior are Polymetal 2.5 S Premium Plus, black with
brown, at **O'Daniel (134 mi, $31,707, 14,354 mi)** and **Neil Huffman
(196 mi, $31,772, 13,846 mi)**. The problem is the comparison:

| | Ohio out the door | Miles |
|---|---|---|
| New 2026 2.5 S Premium at -10%, $1,000 cash, 0% available | $35,559 | 0 |
| CPO 2024 2.5 S Premium Plus, no special APR | $34,742 | 14,354 |
| Hertz 2025 Hybrid Premium Plus, clean report | $35,346 | 22,739 |

Certification saves **$818** against a new car bought properly, for a
two-year-old car with 14,000 miles and no 0% financing (Mazda's CPO rate
support covers the CX-70 and CX-90, not the CX-50). Mazda CPO is real
coverage, a 160-point inspection plus a year of bumper-to-bumper and
7 years/100,000 miles of powertrain from the original in-service date, but
it is not worth $800 of difference when the new car carries the full
warranty. **Buy CPO only under about $30,000**, which today means the
Kerry Mazda car at $27,525 (Polymetal, 20-inch, but black leatherette, so
out of spec on the interior).

The same logic retires the Hertz ex-rentals for this spec: $213 cheaper
than a properly negotiated new car, with 23,000 rental miles.

## The script

1. **This week, one email to eight dealers.** Germain West, Byers,
   Mazda of Dayton, Jeff Schmitt, Kings, Suburban Troy, Dover and Kenny
   Ross. Give the exact spec and name two or three stock numbers from the
   board. Ask for: the selling price before manufacturer cash, the cash
   they will apply, the doc fee, and a total out-the-door figure in
   writing. Say you are buying within two weeks from whoever is lowest.
2. **Skip Germain of Columbus for the written round.** CarEdge asked them
   eight times, by text and email, and got nothing; they quote only in
   person. They hold three Polymetal S Premiums, so visit them last, with
   the other quotes in hand.
3. **Quote the competition by name.** Germain West advertises 8.1% off
   publicly, Dayton 6.9%, Troy 6.2%, Kings 6.1%. Asking a Columbus store
   to beat a written Dayton number is the entire mechanism.
4. **Use the abundance.** Eighty-six in-spec cars within 150 miles. If the
   answer is no, the next dealer has the same car in the same colour.
   Trading 60 miles of driving for $2,000 is the normal outcome here.
5. **Negotiate one number only**, the out-the-door total. Refuse add-ons,
   protection packages, and any doc fee over $398. Ask Jeff Schmitt up
   front about accessories: 17% of their verified quotes carry them,
   averaging $1,598.
6. **Do not mention cash.** Financing is worth money to the dealer; agree
   the price first, then decide how to pay. If Mazda Financial offers a
   finance bonus, take the loan and pay it off.
7. **Timing.** September 30 is a quarter end and worth roughly $300 to
   $700 of extra dealer flexibility, but October's program is unknown and
   could be richer or $1,500 leaner on the Turbo. Get the written quotes
   dated now, ask each dealer to hold the **dealer discount** into early
   October, and let them apply whatever cash exists on the day.
