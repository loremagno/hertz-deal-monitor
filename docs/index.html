Every CX-50 Hybrid on the watch

Aspirational, not gated: open-market listings from Cars.com, 2022 or newer, $47,000 or less, ranked within each model against a curve fitted on that model's listings under the same cap. Nothing here has an AutoCheck read and nothing here alerts. "vs curve" is cheapness relative to the same model's other listings; Cars.com's own deal rating is shown alongside.
Landed = price (doc fee included) + delivery + % Franklin County tax + title and registration. Delivery is the seller's own per-car quote where readable; Rent2Buy cars are collected in person at zero delivery.
vs model is the residual from a hedonic fit of log price on mileage, age, trim tier and a Rent2Buy indicator with model fixed effects, across every watched vehicle; thin models fall back to a Cars.com curve for the same model. It measures cheapness within a model, on asking prices.
Condition is Experian AutoCheck where the seller links one. A reported accident, branded title or odometer flag blocks an alert. Franchise-certified cars pass on the certification and say so. A clean report is not a substitute for a pre-purchase inspection.
