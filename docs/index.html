Cars.com rows carry tax and fees but no delivery, and no history. Change the mileage to see how the ranking moves; that is the question you cannot answer yet.
Landed = price (doc fee included) + delivery + % Franklin County tax + title and registration. Delivery is the seller's own per-car quote where readable; Rent2Buy cars are collected in person at zero delivery.
vs model is a leave-one-out residual: the car's own row is taken out of a fit of log pre-doc-fee price on mileage, model year, the make's trim ladder and a Rent2Buy indicator with model fixed effects across every watched vehicle, and, where a Cars.com curve exists for the model, that prediction is blended in with weight rising in the number of same-model cars we hold ourselves. It measures cheapness within a model, on asking prices.
New cars (the New & CPO Mazda tab) are judged against the manufacturer's sticker rather than the used-car model: "vs sticker" is the dealer's pre-doc-fee advertised price against MSRP, before any manufacturer cash, which is shown separately. There is no history to read on a new car; the factory warranty is the condition. Dealer rows are collected in person, so landed = price + tax + fees.
Dismissing a car (the × at the end of its row) hides it for good in this browser only; it is remembered across refreshes here but not on another device, and it never affects the alerts or the price model. Each table's footer shows how many are hidden and can show them again; ↺ restores one.
Own cost is landed price minus expected resale (the model's own yearly depreciation where a market curve exists, else the default, spread along a front-loaded yearly shape from the car's current age, with the fitted mileage slope) plus fuel at the mileage chosen above, plus a warranty reserve once bumper-to-bumper cover is gone, plus whatever you enter under "assumptions" for maintenance, insurance and the cost of cash. Undiscounted; per year is the total over the holding period. Wait? on the CX-50 tab weighs the expected price cut over the next week (Hertz's markdown rate by days on sale) against the chance the car sells first times what it is worth relative to the market: wait when the first is larger.
