# Negotiating a new or certified CX-50 / CX-70 around Columbus

Written 2026-09-21 from the day's data: Byers Mazda's own feed, Mazda USA's
locator (three Columbus stores, twenty dealers within 300 miles), Cars.com
new and CPO listings, and the September 2026 Mazda programs. Prices move;
the board's "New & CPO Mazda" tab carries the live numbers.

## Where the market sits today

**Programs, valid through September 30, 2026.** CX-50: $1,000 customer cash
on the 2.5 S, $3,000 on the 2.5 Turbo (Turbo, Meridian, Premium Plus),
$1,500 on the Hybrid Premium and Premium Plus; or 0% APR for 36 months
through Mazda Financial. CX-70 3.3 Turbo: $3,500 customer cash, or 0% for
36 months. Loyalty, conquest and military add $500 to $1,000 where they
apply. CPO CX-70 2025-26: 3.9% APR. Byers's feed shows these exact figures
per VIN ("Mfr cash" on the board). October's programs are not published;
they reset on the 1st and are usually similar, sometimes richer as the
2027s approach.

**Model year.** The 2027 CX-50 is a carryover and Mazda had announced
nothing about it as of August. There is no sell-down yet, but Byers's 2.5 S
Premium and 2.5 Turbo units were built in February and March and have sat
six months. Those are the aged units; the Polymetal Gray Meridian arrived
this month and is not.

**What dealers actually take.** Invoice runs 5 to 8% under sticker; holdback
is 2 to 3% of base MSRP on top. Verified 2026 CX-50 transactions average 4
to 5% under sticker before manufacturer cash nationally, 7 to 8% in the

Byers advertises every new car at sticker plus the $398 doc fee plus a $50
delivery line, then shows the manufacturer cash underneath. Its online
price is therefore a starting point, not a quote; its CarEdge grade is A
(no add-ons, quoted price matches the listing, doc fee $387 to $398).
Germain Mazda of Columbus (Morse Rd) charges a $250 doc fee but has refused
written out-the-door quotes eight times in a row to CarEdge's testers;
Germain Mazda West (Georgesville Rd) quotes, charges $398, and shows about
3% off on the one verified deal. The Ohio doc fee cap for 2026 is $398 or
10% of the price, whichever is lower, and Ohio does not tax it. Ohio does
tax the price before manufacturer cash, as I read the rule; check the
buyer's order.

## Targets, before manufacturer cash

Ask for the dealer discount and the cash separately. The cash is Mazda's
money and every dealer passes it through; the discount is the dealer's and
is the only thing being negotiated.

Worked example, the Polymetal Gray / Terracotta Meridian at Byers, sticker
$42,980: at 6.5% off the dealer price is $40,190; less $3,000 cash,
$37,190; tax at 8% on $40,190 is $3,215; doc $398 and title about $100;
out the door about $40,900. Byers's advertised path is $43,429 less $3,000,
tax on $43,031, so about $43,970. The gap is $3,000 and all of it is
dealer discount plus the $50 delivery line.

A certified car has to clear a higher bar. Byers's CPO 2025 CX-50 Turbo
Premium (Polymetal Gray, black with brown, 3,868 miles) asks $36,949 against
an original sticker of $38,275, so 3.5% under original for a used car with
no 0% and no $3,000 cash. A new Turbo Premium Plus at the targets above
lands near $39,500 before tax, with the full warranty and the better
financing. Consider CPO only at 12 to 15% under original sticker, or on the
Hybrid, where the new-car cash is smaller.

## The playbook

1. **Send one written request to eight dealers this week.** Byers, Germain
   West, Germain Columbus, Mazda of Dayton, Jeff Schmitt (Beavercreek),
   Kings and Jake Sweeney (Cincinnati), Suburban Mazda of Troy. Name the
   exact spec (trim, colour, interior, stock number or VIN where the board
   shows one), ask for an itemized out-the-door price in writing with the
   manufacturer cash shown as a line, and say you are buying before
   September 30 from whoever is lowest. The quarter closes on the 30th and
   volume bonuses are decided that week. Dealers that answer in writing
   have committed; the one that will not (Germain Columbus) gets visited
   last, with the other quotes in hand.

2. **Negotiate the out-the-door number, nothing else.** No trade, no
   financing discussed until the price is agreed. Refuse add-ons
   (protection packages, etch, nitrogen), the $50 delivery line, and any
   doc fee above $398. A dealer that moves the discount but adds a
   "market adjustment" or a package has not moved.

3. **Use the aged units.** Byers's two 2.5 Turbo AWD and three Ingot Blue
   2.5 S Premium have been in stock since February and March; the Zircon
   Sand Premium Plus since March 17. Floor-plan interest on a $45,000 car is
   roughly $250 a month, and the manager knows which units are costing him.
   The board's "Days" column is that list. A fresh arrival in your exact
   colours (the gray Meridian) is worth less discount to them; pair the ask
   with the aged unit's price as the reference.

4. **Cash versus 0%.** Mazda's cash and its 0% are normally alternatives,
   not both. On the Meridian: $3,000 cash now, or 0% for 36 months on
   whatever you finance. If you finance $25,000, 36 months at a credit
   union's 6.5% costs about $2,580 in interest, so the cash is slightly
   better and the two are close; finance $30,000 or more and 0% wins. You
   said you would put at least $15,000 down; run both on the agreed price,
   and ask whether the dealer's quote already assumes one of them. A
   quote that nets the cash and then offers 0% is double counting.

5. **Sequence.** Written quotes Monday to Wednesday; counter the best two
   on Thursday with the targets above; sign on Saturday the 27th or Monday
   the 29th, not on the 30th, when the desk is busiest and least
   flexible. If nothing reaches the target, the same request goes out
   again on October 6 with the new program figures.

Byers's new and certified pages are read every four to six hours; Mazda
USA's locator every six, covering Germain and the twenty dealers within
300 miles with colour, interior and sticker; Cars.com adds advertised

any of them sends a push; a markdown of $250 or more into 5% under sticker
sends another. "vs sticker" is before manufacturer cash; "Mfr cash" is the
cash the dealer advertises; "Days" is how long the unit has sat.
